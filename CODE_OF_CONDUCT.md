# Code of Conduct

Trellune is intended to be a respectful learning and contributor community.
Be constructive, assume good intent, welcome different backgrounds and English
levels, and focus criticism on ideas and artifacts rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted personal contact, publication of
private information, or deliberately unsafe handling of learner data are not
acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect
participants and the project.

To report a concern privately, use the security/contact route documented in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Public issue threads are not appropriate for
personal data or security reports.
