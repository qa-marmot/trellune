## Later / deliberately not scheduled

- Day 366–540 curriculum activation
- AI API integrations or automated provider control
- A replacement sync protocol, backup format, or storage schema without a

The roadmap is not a promise. Learning data compatibility, Core evidence
integrity, and privacy take precedence over feature work.
