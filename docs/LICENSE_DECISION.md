# License decision

Trellune is released under the single [MIT License](../LICENSE). It covers the
repository's authored source code, documentation, and authored curriculum.
This intentionally permits reuse and derivatives under the standard MIT notice
requirement; it does not turn curriculum completion into a CEFR certification.

## Why MIT

MIT is a short, well-understood permissive license that keeps the public
snapshot, source, docs, and authored exercises under one straightforward reuse
rule. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions while granting the
license required by the repository's `LICENSE` file.

## Third-party audit

The public tree contains no third-party media assets. The icon set, social SVG,
and screenshots are project-authored or synthetic. Direct npm dependencies were
read from their installed package metadata before release: they declare MIT,
Apache-2.0, MPL-2.0, or an MIT/Apache dual license. Their notices and terms are
preserved by their packages; the project MIT license does not relicense them.
Static English definitions derived from Princeton WordNet 3.0 retain the
required copyright and disclaimer in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

No learner export, audio, pasted provider content, database file, secret, or
production artifact is included in the public snapshot.
