## Delivered post-enrichment profile

| Stage        | Reading progression                             | Writing progression                            | Grammar transfer                                | Integration cadence |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Foundation   | 15 short notices/messages/narratives            | daily short transfer + 15 checkpoint responses | recognition + controlled + short production     | every 6 days        |
| Independent  | 15 paragraph/message/report tasks               | daily 2–4 sentences + 15 structured responses  | transformation + contextual multi-sentence work | every 6 days        |
| Fluency      | 15 reports/opinions with inference              | daily 4–6 sentences + 105–240 word checkpoints | correction + paraphrase + connected explanation | every 6 days        |
| B2 Challenge | 19 two-paragraph texts + 4 long-form challenges | daily 5–8 sentences + 140–250 word checkpoints | correction + qualification + argument transfer  | every 5 days        |

Every day now has productive Grammar, productive Vocabulary, Writing, authored feedback and an
in-session retry path. Reading is rotated across 64 standard checkpoints plus seven 408–457-word
long-form or multi-text challenges rather than presented as a daily long task. Vocabulary practice
uses bounded D+1/D+3/D+7/D+21 curriculum reuse without changing SRS or acquisition counts. The
current integrated Graduation assessment requires reading and writing evidence, one evidence note
per required skill and a complete score profile; older attempts remain valid in their original scope.

## Dependency and outcome after enrichment

| Outcome               | Bundle-native evidence after enrichment | Remaining dependency                         |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Grammar transfer      | Daily, stage-graded open production     | Optional ChatGPT feedback                    |
| Productive vocabulary | Daily context/paraphrase + prior recall | Optional ChatGPT feedback                    |
| Reading               | 64 authored checkpoint texts            | No external service                          |
| Writing               | Daily short output + 64 checkpoints     | No external service                          |
| Spoken production     | No equivalent static interaction        | ChatGPT Voice for intended live evidence     |
| Listening             | No stable bundled audio                 | ChatGPT Voice; delivery quality varies       |
| Interaction/repair    | Written proxy in some labs              | ChatGPT Voice for spontaneous evidence       |
| Pronunciation         | No reliable browser-only evidence       | ChatGPT Voice/human observation when audible |

The Core session import is still required on all `365/365` days. Voice dependency therefore remains
100% at the day-completion level. At the skill-family level, five of seven original declared families
were Voice-only (`71.4%`); after adding reading and writing as formal skills plus bundle-native
grammar/vocabulary practice, five of nine remain primarily Voice-only (`55.6%`). This reduction is
material but is not presented as removal of Voice dependency.

## Descriptor conclusion

The bundled tasks now exercise supporting views, pros/cons, trade-offs, counterargument,
qualification, writer stance, written inference, summarisation, paraphrase, report/email writing and
180–250 word integrated responses. They make an integrated B2 estimate testable. They do not make
full CEFR B2 the default outcome: quantity of input, natural-speed listening reliability, feedback
quality and learner performance remain limiting variables.
