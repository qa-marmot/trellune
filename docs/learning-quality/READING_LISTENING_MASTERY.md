Each challenge requires an uninterrupted gist pass, two authored evidence questions, and one extended response. Feedback identifies meaning-bearing points, source clues, and a plausible misunderstanding; it is never exact-string grading. Four challenges explicitly combine two source roles or texts. Daily workload remains rotational: the maximum bundle-authored Non-Voice load is 43 minutes, and long-form days replace an otherwise unscheduled Lab rather than stacking on one.

## Stage Voice coaching

Every generated Core prompt now carries a `VOICE_COACHING` block derived from the curriculum day. The same rules are documented in Project Sources.

- Day 1–30: short, slow start; one later natural-speed check; choices or a sentence starter when blocked.
- Day 31–90: normal speed on familiar topics; one initial listening turn; main-idea check and learner-led clarification.
- Day 91–180: longer normal-speed turns; reasons/examples, one or two follow-ups, summary and clarification.
- Day 181–270: controlled near-natural speed; key point, simple inference, paraphrase and two or three follow-ups.
- Day 271–365: near-natural speed; stance, implication, alternative perspective, summary, spontaneous follow-up and repair.

Repeated playback is deliberately limited. When comprehension fails, the coach shortens and slows the turn instead of repeatedly replaying identical wording. The product requests role and speaking-style variation at the B2 Challenge stage but does not claim to control the actual ChatGPT Voice speaker or audio quality.
